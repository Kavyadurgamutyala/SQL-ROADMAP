INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

1. What is COUNT()?

Counts total rows.

2. What is SUM()?

Returns total sum.

3. What is AVG()?

Returns average.

4. What is MIN()?

Returns minimum value.

5. What is MAX()?

Returns maximum value.

6. What is GROUP BY?

Groups rows with common values.

7. Why use Aggregate Functions?

To perform calculations on multiple rows.

8. What is ROUND()?

Rounds numerical values.
